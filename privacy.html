🔒 A clear note about our little site

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the Charlotte & Luke website handles information when we use it. It is a personal shared website, operated by its site owner, Luke.

Please know: this website uses both private storage and a public GitHub repository. Some shared entries saved through the site may be readable by anyone on the internet. Please do not enter anything into the site that we need to keep confidential, with one exception: Our little safe, which is encrypted in the browser before it is saved.

Information the site handles

  • Shared entries: notes, calendar events, quiet-day entries, memories, rules, profile data, and TikTok plans may be stored as files in the site's public GitHub repository. This means those saved files can be viewed by the public, and edits may remain in GitHub's version history.
  • New gallery photos: originals are uploaded to the shared Google Photos account. The site stores Google Photos media IDs and our album, uploader, and upload-date details in Cloudflare's Durable Object. Temporary Google Photos media URLs are used to display photos and are not saved. Existing photos may remain in Backblaze B2 or the public GitHub repository.
  • Question Night, presence, bathroom check-ins, settings, and video list: these records are stored by the site's Cloudflare application services and are used to provide the shared features.
  • Sign-in: the site uses a sign-in name and session cookie to keep the shared areas available to signed-in users. It may keep a selected profile name and preferences in the browser's local storage.

Our little safe

Our little safe keeps the account names and passwords we choose to share. It is encrypted in the browser with a passphrase we pick together. The passphrase and the key made from it never leave the browser and are never sent to Cloudflare, GitHub, or anyone else. The site only stores an encrypted blob, which is scrambled with PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM before it is saved.

Because the passphrase is never stored anywhere, it cannot be reset or recovered. If we forget it, the only option is to start the safe again with a new passphrase, which erases what was saved. Anyone using a device that is already signed in and who knows the passphrase can read the safe, so it is worth locking it and keeping the passphrase to ourselves. The safe page also requires the normal site sign-in.

The safe's page code is served by this site. Someone who can change that code could change how the passphrase is handled, so the encryption protects the saved data at rest and in transit, not against a tampered website.

Google Photos and YouTube

When we choose Connect our shared Google Photos, the browser asks for permission to add photos to the shared Google Photos account and read only photos created through this website. The website does not read the account's other Google Photos. The browser uploads and retrieves photos directly with Google; the temporary access token stays in browser memory and is not sent to or stored by our website. We store the Google media IDs and our album labels so the website can find those app-created items again. We both need to connect the same shared Google account to see the same photos.

Google Photos API data is used only to provide the visible gallery feature, and its use adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google may process photos under its own terms and privacy policy. We can remove photos from the shared Google Photos account in Google Photos; the website may then show an unavailable item until its gallery record is removed.

If we choose to upload a video, the browser asks us to authorize Google for YouTube upload and read access. The app uses that authorization to upload the video and confirm its YouTube details. We do not receive or store our Google password. The app does not store the temporary Google access token after the upload/verification request.

Videos uploaded through the site are set to Unlisted. They are not intended to appear in YouTube search, but anyone who has a video's link can watch or share it. YouTube processes and hosts the video under its own terms and privacy policy. We store the video ID and related display details in the app.

Google's access can be reviewed or removed from our Google Account security settings. Removing access prevents future authorized uploads until we authorize again.

Service providers and external content

The site is hosted and operated using Cloudflare. It uses Google Photos for new gallery photo uploads, Backblaze B2 for older gallery files, and GitHub for source code and some shared records. YouTube/Google handles video uploads and playback. Spotify and TikTok links or embeds may connect to those services. Those providers process information under their own policies when we use their services.

How information is used and kept

Information is used to run the features we choose to use together. The site is not designed to sell personal information or show advertising. Records remain in their storage until they are removed through the site or by the site owner. Removing or editing a file in GitHub may not remove earlier copies from the repository's history. Google Photos images, YouTube videos, and older B2 photos may also need to be deleted from their respective services separately.

Contact and changes

For a privacy question or a request about information on this site, contact the site owner using the support contact shown in the site's Google OAuth app information. This policy may be updated if the site's features or storage change; the effective date above will be updated when that happens.
